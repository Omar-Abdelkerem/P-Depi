The Story

I rejected medicine to write code.

Hey, I'm Omar 👋 Three years ago, I packed my life into a bag and left home for a place I'd chosen over every "smart" option on the table. People kept asking if I was sure. I was. I'm from Sohag, and I could have become a doctor, or chosen a traditional engineering major. I said no to both, because I wanted Computer Science — and not just anywhere, but Zewail City.

I've always needed to know "how does this actually work," and code is that curiosity with no limits. I adore this university, and leaving Sohag to be part of it is a choice I'd make again tomorrow.

Since then I've shipped 7 real products, joined Egypt's national DEPI program, and started building production backend for a live real-estate client. I chose this path over the safe ones, so I treat "it works on my machine" as unfinished.
